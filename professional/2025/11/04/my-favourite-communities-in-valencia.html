    My favourite communities in Valencia

    1 minute read

    Recently, I embarked on a mission to explore Valencia’s vibrant tech community, diving into meetups and discovering some incredible new groups! I’m excited to share my top picks that capture the spirit of innovation across AI, Data Science, Machine Learning, and Product here in Valencia. The flag emojis indicate the language typically used in the meetup.

    1. AI Engineers Valencia 🇬🇧: Weekly informal meetups in a bar, where I made valuable connections right from my first attendance! 🤖+🍻
    2. AI Tinkerers Valencia 🇬🇧: The newest kid on the block, I loved their powerful format for project demos and instant, valuable feedback! 🚀
    3. Python Valencia 🇪🇸: The ultimate spot to connect with Valencia’s vibrant Python community. Expect engaging talks and fantastic vibes! 🐍
    4. PyData Valencia 🇪🇸: Data scientists, keep an eye out for this exciting new community! With a great team behind it, I’m already looking forward to the next meetup! 🐍+📈
    5. DataBeers València 🇪🇸: Bringing people together to talk about data since 2016 – this was the first community I discovered in the city! 📊+🍻
    6. Awakatech 🇪🇸: Typically a two-talk combo focusing on product & engineering. I enjoy this mix almost as much as the guacamole for networking afterward! 🥑
    7. Valencia Codes 🇬🇧: Offering the most diverse range of topics for Valencia’s tech community! 💡
    8. ProductTank Valencia 🇬🇧: While aimed at product managers, engineers with a product mindset will absolutely love it too! ❤️
    9. Red IA y datos Morvedre 🇪🇸: Last but not least! Not in Valencia, but in Sagunto, where I live. This community brings cutting-edge data science to a backdrop of ancient Roman ruins! Over 2000 years of progress! 🏛️🤖
